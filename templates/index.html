PDF Crawler

By Praveen Kumar

Enter a landing page URL. The tool will crawl child pages under that section, download PDFs, and return a ZIP containing the PDFs and an Excel audit log.

This web app uses Render's free to use plan. As a result, there are some usage limitations that come with this:

  • Only nsw.gov.au urls are allowed.
  • The app is set to timeout at 5 minutes, so don't go crawling entire departments, ex: all of NESA. If your crawl fails, narrow your scope to child pages.
  • Maximum downloadable pdfs are limited to 300. If your pages are very pdf heavy, again, narrow your scope to child pages and work up from there.
  • Maximum pages of a pdf limited to 150. So extra large pdfs will not download.
  • It is likely that if multiple users are concurrently using the app, it may not work. If so, please try again later.
